The Big Picture

The Structure at a Glance

Two separate companies, on purpose. ONE FOUNDATION is the ministry — it owns the church relationships and sustains the sites. Life Wellness Clinic is the clinical company — it owns the therapists, the payer contracts, and every clinical dollar. One agreement (the MSA) connects them.

Payment Flow

Follow the Dollar

Six steps, every month, in this order. Notice that money only ever moves in one direction at each step, and every clinical dollar lands at the clinic first.

A session happens

A therapist meets a client at a church site (or the hub). The client arrived through a pastor's warm hand-off, a ministry-team referral, or the community.

The clinic collects

Insurance reimbursement, co-pay, self-pay — or a session subsidized by a church benevolence fund. Every clinical dollar lands at the PLLC. Nothing clinical is ever paid to the ministry.

The clinic pays the therapist

The therapist receives their net session split through clinic payroll, under their Platform Agreement with the clinic.

The clinic covers its operations

EHR, billing, credentialing, HIPAA compliance, the central scheduler and receptionist — the clinical machine the clinic owns and runs.

Month-end: the ministry invoices

ONE FOUNDATION bills the clinic one monthly fee under the MSA — priced to cover the ministry staff who sustain the church sites. Never a percentage of revenue.

The ministry reinvests

Church relationships, site setup and sustainment, marketing, recruiting support, therapist welfare check-ins — so more sites open and more people get care.

Division of Labor

Who Does What

A clean split with no overlap: the ministry sustains the sites; the clinic delivers the care. If a task touches a patient, a payer, or PHI — it belongs to the clinic. If it touches a church relationship or site sustainment — it belongs to the ministry.

ONE FOUNDATION — The Ministry
sustains the church sites · owns the church relationships
  • Church relationship management — holds every Church Partnership Agreement
  • Site setup & local administrative protocols at each church
  • Warm introductions between therapists and existing ministry teams
  • Marketing & community outreach for the sites
  • Therapist recruiting support & general coordination
  • Ongoing site sustainment — keeping each location healthy
  • Regular welfare check-ins with the therapists
  • Referee role: sets and enforces service standards clinic(s) must meet at church sites
Never touches: clinical decisions, patient records/PHI, payer contracts, billing, or therapist employment.
LIFE WELLNESS CLINIC — The Clinic
delivers the care · owns everything clinical
  • Contracts the therapists (licensed, future pre-licensed & supervisor agreements)
  • Credentials therapists with insurance payers
  • Clinical onboarding & supervision structure
  • Provides the EHR; ensures HIPAA / PHI compliance
  • Central scheduling system with receptionist — supports therapists, never dictates their schedules
  • Billing & collections from all payment sources
  • Therapist payroll (net session splits)
  • All clinical judgment, protocols & care decisions
Retains 100% of clinical revenue and 100% clinical control. Buys the minisry's administrative services for a predictable fee.
The Paper Trail

The Governing Documents

Five document types hold the whole structure together. Each one connects exactly two parties and does exactly one job.

AgreementBetweenWhat it does
Church Partnership Agreement ONE FOUNDATION ↔ each church The ministry owns the church relationship. Defines what the church provides (space, warm introductions, ministry-team access) and what the ministry sustains at the site. Churches partner with a ministry — never with a business.
Ministry Services Agreement (MSA) ONE FOUNDATION ↔ the clinic
 The one bridge between the two companies. Lists the administrative services the ministry provides, sets the flat monthly fee, and sets the service standards the clinic must meet at every church site the ministry brings it into.
Therapist Platform Agreements Clinic ↔ each therapist Licensed, future pre-licensed, and supervisor versions. The clinic — not the ministry — contracts, credentials, onboards, and pays every therapist.
Business Associate Agreement (BAA) Clinic ↔ therapists & vendors HIPAA protections around PHI. All patient data lives inside the clinic's compliance perimeter; the ministry sits entirely outside it.
Payer Contracts Clinic ↔ insurance payers The clinic holds every payer contract in its own name. Reimbursements flow only to the clinic — the ministry never appears anywhere in the revenue cycle.
The Design Logic

Why It's Built This Way

FOR THE CHURCHES

A ministry at the table, not a vendor

Pastors partner with ONE FOUNDATION — a ministry with a shared mission — nearly no objections relative to a healthcare business. The ministry holds that trust acting as the key matchmaker and mediator.

FOR THE CLINIC

Full clinical control, zero site burden

The clinic keeps 100% of clinical revenue and every clinical decision. It gains ready-made church sites, warm referral pipelines, and site sustainment — fee schedule subject to attorney MSA review.

FOR THE MISSION

The ministry is the referee

Because the ministry owns the church agreements, it is responsible to hold the clinic/therapist accountable to the service standards agreed to in the church's partnership agreement mirrored in the MSA (positioning statement).

Compliance Guardrails

The Lines We Never Cross

  • The ministry fee is likely headcount based and/or metered — never a percentage of clinical revenue (avoids fee-splitting exposure under Tennessee law). A non-parasitic mechanism TDB in the MSA to ensure fees never exceed site net income. Each month's invoice is backed by documented services rendered.
  • The ministry never touches clinical decisions, patient care, PHI, payer contracts, credentialing decisions, or therapist hiring and firing (different from site placement/removal) — those live at the clinic, full stop.
  • All clinical revenue — insurance, co-pays, self-pay, benevolence subsidies — is paid only to the clinic.
  • Every definitive agreement receives healthcare-attorney review before signature. During proof of concept, I research and draft as much as I can but will look to find free or affordable attorney review ASAP.
