Missional Wellness Services LLC (in formation)

Professional. Credentialed. Gospel-Centered.

Missional Wellness Defined

Missional Wellness intentionally positions counselors, who are Christians, in the public therapy marketplace where lost people are actively looking for relief. It recognizes the national shortage of providers and intentionally delivers a service that is in demand while creating opportunities to introduce people to Jesus and the life of the local church. Simultaneously, Missional Wellness sustains and multiplies marketplace missionaries by delivering wellness services competitively among the broad therapy marketplace ensuring it maintains an appealing value proposition to the general population.

Our Vision

We exist to be the definitive missional therapy network in America carrying the Gospel and pointing people into the local church — delivering professional, community-embedded mental health care through world-class therapists who personally know that lasting life wellness is built on One Foundation (1 Cor. 3) and flows from a living relationship with Jesus Christ. We will build the largest unified Gospel-powered mental health workforce in the country, while meeting people exactly where society is already looking — one partnering therapist, one partnering church, and one foundation to build on together.

Our Mission

We recruit, develop, deploy, and sustain world-class therapists who personally know lasting life wellness comes from knowing and loving the author and sustainer of life, Jesus Christ — who have the same talent and discretion as mental health professionals to be bold with their faith when there is opportunity, and to be clandestine Gospel agents, prayer warriors, and esteemed members of their communities when there is not — who consistently ready themselves for opportunity while they faithfully deliver professional, clinical care, in obedience to the Great Commission.

Why the Church?

The local church is God's design for delivering the Gospel and caring for whole people. It is the most strategic platform for sustainable, relational, community-embedded mental health care.

America's mental health crisis will not get better without the local church taking an active role. This is an opportunity to collaborate with Christian Therapists who carry the true solution to where people are already looking in the therapy marketplace.

We are leading the missional wellness ministry movement that positions Spirit-filled therapists as a natural extension of the church's care — mission-aligned but marketplace facing, and quietly showing up for the church and local community year after year in a sustainable, not burdensome way, by building on one foundation, Jesus Christ (1 Cor. 3:10–11).

This isn't designed just to comfort Christians but to point the lost to Christ. The Spirit of the Lord is upon us — to bind up the brokenhearted, to proclaim freedom to the captives, and to comfort all who mourn. Isaiah 61

What are we really trying to accomplish?

The Problem

1 in 5 Americans lives with mental illness, yet only 26% of mental health needs are being met. More than 122 million Americans live in areas with a shortage of mental health professionals1, and HRSA projects a deficit of nearly 90,000 counselors by 20372. The need is not slowing down. The system is not catching up.

The Deeper Truth

A therapy company that does not carry the Gospel is managing symptoms at best, not delivering transformation. A Christian Counseling practice that openly markets themselves as Christians and declines to take insurance payments may be meeting the needs of the saints, but is missing an opportunity to engage the lost. We quietly carry the power of Christ into the marketplace for genuine change. "If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; the old has gone, the new has come." (2 Cor. 5:17) We are not a loud evangelical movement. Rather, we are intentionally and competitively meeting a tremendous need — professionally credentialed, marketplace-aligned — but carrying a deeper truth that is foundational to human flourishing.

We have this treasure in earthen vessels, to show that the surpassing power belongs to God and not to us. We don't apologize for it.

1 Kaiser Family Foundation, Mental Health Care Health Professional Shortage Areas, 2024. kff.org
2 Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA), State of the Behavioral Health Workforce, November 2024. bhw.hrsa.gov

The Opportunity

In addition to taking on an existential threat to America, you get an embedded, credentialed, Gospel-centered therapist on your campus — no cost to you, we walk together through administrative setup details, no new ministry to manage. We handle everything. You provide space and a warm welcome. We become a specialized extension of your care services, and together we begin making a lasting difference in America's mental health crisis.

Where This Goes

The Denning and Delay families have come together with a God-sized dream to transform the mental health market and deliver a sustainable force of Gospel-equipped, clinical professionals across America. This is a missional wellness company, building teamwork with ministries for maximum impact. In a future phase, we are designing to promote Christian Mental Health Service Provider paid residencies so that mission-minded Christians have a clear path from graduate school to licensure — helping solve the provider shortage across the nation by producing more Christian therapists.

ONE FOUNDATION | Essential Beliefs

About God, People, and the Only Foundation for Lasting Life Wellness

We are not here to debate what divides. We are decidedly working together to build on one foundation, Jesus Christ (1 Cor. 3:10–11). Day by day, we carry the peace and power of the Gospel into every session, showing up — credentialed, professional, and praying. With clients who are open, we share the core truths of the Gospel in plain language as stated below, and refer back to the church's ministries to continue the conversation. With all clients, we humbly carry this treasure knowing the power of God is in His people and the community a person needs to flourish is right in your church.

God knows everything about you, loves you, and has a purpose for you.

God made you on purpose, for a purpose, and He loves you completely. Before the anxious thoughts, before the wounds, before the weight of every hard season, God knew you and had a plan for you to flourish. The life He intends for you is one of genuine peace, wholeness, and relationship with Him.

John 3:16  •  John 10:10  •  Genesis 1:31

Sin has separated every person from God — and from the life He designed.

Every person who has ever lived has chosen their own way over God's way. The Bible calls this sin — and its consequence is separation from God, the very source of life and peace. This separation explains what every honest person already knows: something is broken. In ourselves, in our relationships, in the world. No amount of therapy, achievement, or self-improvement can close a gap that is, at its root, spiritual. We were made for closeness with God, and without Him, nothing else fully satisfies or fully heals. We were made to live according to God's design. Anything outside of His design only adds to our pain and to others. Good therapy helps. But it was never designed to do what only God can do.

Romans 3:23  •  Romans 6:23  •  Isaiah 59:2

Jesus Christ is God's plan to restore and redeem us.

God did not leave us in our separation. He crossed it. Jesus Christ, fully God and fully human, lived the life we could not live, died the death we deserved, and rose from the grave — defeating sin, death, and the power of everything that has broken us. He is the only bridge between a holy God and a broken humanity. Not one of many ways. The way. Salvation is not earned, achieved, or inherited. It is received — freely, by anyone who will believe.

John 14:6  •  Romans 5:8  •  1 Corinthians 15:3–4  •  Ephesians 2:8–9

Each person must personally receive Christ to experience lasting life wellness.

Knowing about Jesus is not the same as knowing Jesus. At some moment, every person must respond. To receive Christ is to personally acknowledge your need, place your trust in His death and resurrection as payment for your sin, and invite Him to take the throne of your life. This is not a religious ritual. It is the beginning of a living relationship with God — and the only foundation on which lasting peace, wholeness, and life transformation are built.

John 1:12  •  Revelation 3:20  •  Romans 10:9–10  •  2 Corinthians 5:17  •  Romans 8:12–39
