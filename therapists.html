The Challenge

Your community and local churches are
carrying some heavy stuff.

We partner and collaborate with existing ministries to provide professional clinical care, when and where evidenced-based therapeutic techniques can accelerate hope and healing. Today nearly 1 in 5 Americans are dealing with depression, trauma, addiction, grief, and relationship crisis often leading to a mental health diagnosis. They are looking for a licensed therapist but we want them to have so much more. Nothing replaces Jesus and the community of the local church.

Would You Consider Partnering With Us

To build a self-sustaining,
vocational wellness ministry?

We believe the local church is God's design for delivering the Gospel and caring for whole people. When you join us, you'll be embedded into a local ministry team to sustainably deliver clinical care while building a meaningful career with a Gospel impact. You're joining a community of likeminded people, with an incredible opportunity to contribute specialized skills, all with competitive pay, significant autonomy, and schedule flexibility!

01

Connect

Let's have a conversation — no commitment. We want to understand your goals, needs, and develop a rewarding vocational ministry career with you.

02

Prepare

We prepare you to serve in one of our partnering churches with our partnering clinic supporting all your administrative functions including insurance credentialing, EHR, availability, scheduling, a receptionist, medical billing, and payroll.

03

Embed

We will provide you with an orientation to our local church partner and a warm introduction to their collaborative ministry teams. You can then be booked for sessions on site and are fully supported through our provider clinic. You are now an embedded mental health professional sustainably delivering specialized care with eternal impact among a diverse team sharing the mission.

04

Grow

As ONE FOUNDATION continues to grow and meaningfully cares for faith communities and their neighbors, so does its ability to care for our partnering therapists. We're here to serve you too. And we intend to get better at it in every way!
