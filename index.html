The Challenge

Your community and congregation are
carrying some heavy stuff.

We partner and collaborate with your existing ministries to provide professional clinical care, when and where evidenced-based therapeutic techniques can accelerate hope and healing. They are looking for a licensed therapist but we want them to have so much more. Nothing replaces Jesus and the community of a local church.
